valence_shifters.csv — Provenance Note
=======================================

The token lists and integer type-coding scheme (1 = negator, 2 = amplifier,
3 = de-amplifier, 4 = adversative conjunction) in this file are derived from
the valence_shifters_table bundled with the sentimentr R package:

  Rinker, T. W. (2021). sentimentr: Calculate text polarity sentiment.
  University at Buffalo/SUNY. https://github.com/trinker/sentimentr
